1. Opening & Meeting Purpose Weekly Pastoral Oversight Review
The weekly Pastors Council meeting was chaired by Snr. Overseer Bett and opened with prayer by Pst. Kelvin Bush. The council reviewed departmental progress, service coordination, finances and cluster reporting. Where improvements were needed, the council agreed practical follow-up actions for the responsible teams.
2. Departmental Review Updates and Agreed Next Steps
Area Update Recorded Agreed Next Step
Worship Team Three new worshippers joined service, with two expected to continue: a violinist/worshipper and a keyboardist. Contributions toward new gowns are ongoing, though progress is slow. Continue gown mobilization and include serving members, absentees and additions in future reports to support clear growth review.
Protocol & Visitors Care Follow-up has been strengthened and active-member information has been shared with cluster leadership to assist placement. Continue member and visitor follow-up, fellowship with assigned visitors, and share graduate visitors with clusters.
New Believers Class Graduates visitors being integrated into church membership should receive the foundation provided through the new believers class. Protocol members will establish interest, share class timing and confirm likely attendance by Saturday evening so pastors can plan the class.
Clusters Department Pst. Moses reported receipt of 12 of 22 cluster reports. Women fellowship clusters submitted all reports; some other groups remain incomplete. Two pastoral cluster groups still require leaders. Share cluster rosters and leaders in text form, allow pastors to confirm placement, appoint missing leaders and follow up outstanding reports.
Sunday School & Children The fellowship is expected to provide weekly reporting even when the lead pastor is unavailable. Concern was raised about children playing along the corridors with a trolley after service. Teachers will maintain weekly reporting. Parents will be reminded to supervise their children after service, with support from ushers and security.
Departmental Reporting Reports from some departments, including security, keyboardists and violinists, were not available during the meeting. Regular departmental meetings have also not been consistent. Departments will resume team deliberations and submit concise weekly reports containing progress, needs and matters requiring council attention.
3. Service Operations & Finance Updates and Coordination Matters
Area Update Recorded Agreed Next Step
Health & Sanitation The council addressed the need for consistent handwashing and sanitizing, especially among leaders, in line with the guidance communicated to the church. All pastors and leaders will comply and model the required practice. Pst. Dickson will circulate precautionary guidance before the next service.
Ushers & Offering Flow Ushers raised member absenteeism and noted that the time taken before prayer for collected offering can require them to stand with it for too long. Ushering leadership will follow up its members. Pastors on Duty and church leadership will coordinate a smoother, more timely offering-prayer flow.
Finance & Rent Pastors were reminded of the monthly Ksh. 450 allocation, while rent remains due by the 7th of each month. Rent committees will prioritize rent mobilization. Pastors will sustain the monthly allocation and communicate where contribution challenges arise.
Equipment Needs The keyboard payment is overdue and needs new direction. IT and media also reported the recurring cost of hiring a camera for Sunday services. Finance will assess the camera provision and submit possible ways forward on the keyboard matter for leadership direction.
Sunday Provisions Clean water contributions were progressing well. The council also reviewed disposable cups, flowers and men's provisions required for the coming service. Overseers and PODs will coordinate water and cups; Elder Joel will follow up men's provisions; Pst. Harrison will coordinate flowers.

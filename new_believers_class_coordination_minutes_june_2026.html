1. Opening & Meeting Purpose New Believers Classes Setup
The New Believers Classes Coordination Team convened on Monday, 2nd June 2026 to establish the structure, curriculum and logistics for the new believers classes. The meeting focused on defining the lesson topics, assigning teaching responsibilities to each pastor, and coordinating the onboarding of current visitors and graduated members into the classes.
2. Curriculum & Weekly Lesson Plan Four-Week Class Structure
The team agreed to structure the new believers classes into a four-week programme, with each week covering a distinct foundational topic. Teaching assignments were distributed among the pastors as follows:
Week Topic / Lesson Assigned Teacher Scope & Coverage
1 Introduction & Salvation Pst. Dr. Dickson Omondi Foundation of faith, understanding salvation, what it means to be born again and the journey of a new believer in the Ministry of Repentance and Holiness.
2 Repentance & Baptism Snr. Pst. Zachary What is Repentance and the significance of water baptism.
3 The Holy Spirit & The Word Snr. Pst. Zachary The role and power of the Holy Spirit in the believer's life, the authority of the Word of God, and how to study and apply Scripture daily.
4 Marriage Sanctification & Service Overseer Nicholas Owino Marriage sanctification in the ministry, guidance for youth desiring marriage, and how to serve faithfully within the Ministry of Repentance and Holiness.
3. Key Deliberations & Resolutions Coordination & Logistics
  • Onboarding of Current Visitors: It was resolved that Pst. Kelvin Wachiye will work to ensure that all current visitors and existing members who are due for the new believers classes are enrolled and added to the class by Sunday.
  • Visitor Handover After Follow-up: Pastors who are assigned visitors weekly will hand over those visitors to the new believers classes after four weeks of fellowship with the assigned visitors, completing the follow-up cycle.
  • Learning Materials — Books & Pens: It was noted that believers attending the class will require notebooks and pens. The team agreed to procure five 96-page hardcover exercise books and one packet of pens (a mixture of blue and red).
  • Fasting & Prayer: The team resolved that there will be a period of fasting and prayer dedicated to the leaders who will be handling the new believers classes, seeking God's guidance.
  • Coordination with Current Class Coordinator: Pst. Kelvin Wachiye was tasked to coordinate with Coordinator Kevin Oloo Mmbaga, who has been assigned as the coordinator for the current running class, to obtain the remaining member details and assist with WhatsApp coordination to onboard them into the new believers classes.
